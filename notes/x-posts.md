# X Posts

Templates and formats for documenting this project publicly on X/Twitter.
The goal is authentic technical documentation, not promotion — not every
post needs to fit a template, and not every post needs to sound like an
announcement.

## Post formats

1. Daily learning update
2. Paper breakdown
3. Implementation update
4. Experiment result
5. Failure post
6. Weekly recap
7. Monthly recap
8. Major milestone
9. Collaboration update
10. Research publication
