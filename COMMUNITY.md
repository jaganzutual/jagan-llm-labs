# Community

This repository is a personal, ongoing journey with no fixed end date, but
it's built in public and built to be inspected, questioned, and (ideally)
collaborated on.

## Ways to get involved

- **Discuss an experiment** — open an issue on any `experiments/` or
  `papers/` folder with questions or critiques.
- **Reproduce results** — rerun an experiment on your own hardware and
  report what you find, including differences.
- **Suggest papers** — propose additions to the roadmap with a short
  rationale for why it fits the current phase.
- **Point out mistakes** — math errors, incorrect claims, bad benchmarks,
  broken links. These are wanted, not just tolerated.
- **Propose experiments** — if a paper folder is missing an obvious
  follow-up experiment, suggest it.
- **Contribute code** — bug fixes, cleaner implementations, better tests.
- **Collaborate on research** — once Phase 6 (original research) begins,
  genuine collaborators are welcome.

## What this repo will not do

- Fabricate collaborations, mentions, or interactions that didn't happen.
- Claim a small-scale experiment reproduces a frontier-scale result.
- Call a plain implementation "research."

## Ground rules

Be technical, be specific, and be honest about uncertainty. Disagreement
about results or methodology is welcome as long as it's substantiated.
